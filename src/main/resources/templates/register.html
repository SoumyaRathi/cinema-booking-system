Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act)
Data Privacy Act of 2012

Republic Act 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, is a law that aims to protect all forms of information, be it private, personal, or sensitive. It is meant to cover both natural and juridical persons involved in the processing of personal information.
